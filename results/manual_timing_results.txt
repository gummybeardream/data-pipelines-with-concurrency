Environment
macOS: 26.6.2
Go: go1.27.1 darwin/arm64
Model: MacBookPro17,1
Processor: Apple M1
Memory: 16 GiB

Method
The executable was built once before measurement.
Each mode received one unrecorded warm-up run.
Five paired sequential and concurrent runs were measured with the zsh time command.
The elapsed total value was used for comparison.
